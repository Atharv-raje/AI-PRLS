# Chapter map — TherapyEd Chapter 1: Certification of the OTR (team's own map)

Purpose: tell the tutor what Chapter 1 covers so it can point students to the
right place and generate on-topic questions. This map is the team's original
summary of topic coverage — it contains no textbook text.

## Topics covered in Chapter 1 (pointer targets)

- Credentialing agencies: what NBCOT is, how certification differs from state
  licensure, state regulatory boards, the licensure compact effort.
- Exam content: the practice-analysis basis of the exam, the four domains and
  their weightings, how items are developed and reviewed.
- Exam format: single-response items and six-option scenario sets.
- Application procedures: eligibility, the character review, the authorization
  to test letter, name-matching with identification.
- Testing accommodations: ADA-based eligibility, the application process,
  comfort aids allowed without pre-approval.
- Exam day: preparation, test-center procedures, breaks, prohibited items.
- Test-taking strategies: pacing, answer-changing discipline, eliminating
  unsafe or absolute options, managing anxiety.
- After the exam: score reporting, retake planning.

## Typical question angles for this chapter (blueprint for the Question Maker)

- Mostly Domain 4 (professional/practice management) angles: what a candidate
  or new practitioner should do in a certification, licensure, or ethics
  situation.
- Good stems: a graduate planning their application timeline; a candidate with
  a documented disability deciding whether to request accommodations; a
  test-taker deciding how to handle remaining time; a new OTR moving to a
  state and confusing certification with licensure.
- Emphasize the distinction: NBCOT certification is national and voluntary in
  form, while the legal permission to practice comes from the state board.

## Standard textbook pointer for this chapter

"Chapter 1 of your TherapyEd book — certification, exam format, and
test-taking strategies."
